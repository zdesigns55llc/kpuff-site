# How to update your website (draft: finalize after the test run)

You can change your wording, services, phone number and photos yourself. You do not need
to touch any code. The design stays the same.

## To change something

1. Open the editor and sign in (your GitHub login).
2. Open **Website content**.
3. Change the text or photo you want.
4. Save.
5. Wait a couple of minutes, then refresh your website to see the change.

## Things you can change

- Wording: the badge, headings, short sentences, and your services with their descriptions
- Photos: your profile photo and the photos of your work
- Your booking phone number and your Messenger link
- The Google title and description

## Photo tips

- Use a clear, well-lit photo. A photo about 1200 pixels wide is plenty.
- Keep each photo under about 500 KB so the site stays fast on phones.
